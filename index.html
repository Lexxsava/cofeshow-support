Cofeshow Support

Cofeshow turns your photos and music into video slideshows on iPhone, iPad and Mac. If something doesn’t work as expected, check the answers below or write to us.

Contact

Email: support@cofeshow.com

Please mention your device, its system version and, if possible, attach a screenshot. We usually reply within a few business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a watermark on my video?

The free version of Cofeshow adds a small watermark to exported videos. You can remove it:

  • from one project — with a one-time purchase that covers 10 exports of that project;
  • from all videos — with a monthly or yearly subscription.

Cofeshow LT for Mac is a paid app and never adds a watermark.

I already paid, but the watermark is still there

Open the purchase screen or Settings in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase. On Mac, the full version of Cofeshow bought earlier is recognized automatically.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions. A subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. See also Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A theme won’t open or keeps loading

Themes are downloaded the first time you use them, so an internet connection is needed once. Check your connection and try again. After downloading, a theme works offline.

Where are my videos on Mac?

Finished videos are saved to the Movies folder in your home folder: Video slideshow - Cofeshow for Cofeshow LT and Video slideshow - Cofeshow WM for the free version.

I updated the Mac app — where are my old projects?

Projects made with earlier versions of Cofeshow for Mac open in the new version with their photos and music. If photos or songs were moved or renamed since, the app shows which files are missing and lets you locate them again.

What do I need to run Cofeshow?

  • Mac: macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
  • iPhone and iPad: the system version listed on the App Store page.
